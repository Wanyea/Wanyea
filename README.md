### 🔍 About me:
I am a Software Engineer with an MS in Computer Science from the University of Central Florida, where I specialized in Brain-Computer Interfaces and Mixed Reality Engineering <img align="center" src= "https://user-images.githubusercontent.com/61568218/213039435-3d951a60-9c71-473b-bacd-0e68018a6be0.png" width="30">🧠!
</br> Currently I am a Software Engineer at [Foundation Health](https://www.foundationhealth.com/), building automated clinical-decisioning systems for pharmacy care. 💊
</br> Previously I led R&D in BCIs, affective computing, and real-time interactive systems at Universal Creative (NBCUniversal).

---
- 🧠 **What I'm into:** Brain-computer interfaces, EEG signal processing, affective computing, and real-time interactive systems — using neural and physiological signals to make technology respond to people instead of the other way around.

- 🔬 **How that looks in practice:** EEG/ECG/PPG pipelines (Emotiv, OpenBCI, Muse), bandpass/notch filtering, Welch PSD, frontal alpha asymmetry, valence/arousal modeling, and multimodal sensor fusion — plus the Unity/XR side that makes it all interactive.

- 📄 **Published work:** [NeuroGaze: A Hybrid EEG and Eye-Tracking Brain-Computer Interface for Hands-Free Interaction in Virtual Reality](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2025.1695446) — *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 2025.

- 🔭 **Repos I'm contributing to the most right now:** [NeuroGaze](https://github.com/Wanyea/NeuroGaze), [Mindforce-Arena](https://github.com/Wanyea/Mindforce-Arena), [Muse-EEG-MI](https://github.com/Wanyea/Muse-EEG-MI), and [Adventour](https://github.com/Wanyea/Adventour-v2). Please check them all out! 😎

- 👯 **I'm looking to collaborate with:** Anyone passionate and ambitious, willing to challenge expectations in current BCI technologies!

- 💬 **My favorite quote at the moment:** "It's important to have specific dreams. Dream Big. Dream without fear." - Randy Pausch

- 😄 **Pronouns:** He/Him/His
